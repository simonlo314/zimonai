# ZimonAI — creative direction

## Thesis

**Verification is an investigation.** The visitor should not be told that ZimonAI checks supplier claims; the interface should let them move a claim through source, record, comparison, and conclusion.

Audience: independent overseas buyers, FBA and Shopify sellers, small importers, and small trading businesses buying from China. The home page has one job: turn uncertainty about a supplier into a clear request for verification.

## Visual system

- **Bureau ink — `#102228`:** primary text and document structure.
- **Registry blue — `#174A5B`:** links, focus, and active evidence.
- **Archive mist — `#E7ECEB`:** document fields and quiet section shifts.
- **Evidence white — `#F7F8F5`:** main ground; cool rather than nostalgic or cream.
- **Verified — `#147A5A`:** confirmed evidence only.
- **Unresolved — `#A06A16`:** unable-to-verify states.
- **Discrepancy — `#B6463A`:** mismatched records and warnings.

Typography uses a restrained editorial serif for thesis statements, a highly legible sans for explanations, and a mono utility face for dates, sources, references, and statuses. Typography—not decoration—creates the premium character.

## Layout concept

The site alternates between wide editorial statements and precise evidence surfaces. It deliberately avoids repeated three-card grids.

## Signature interactions

1. **Dossier verification:** a clearly fictional supplier file progresses through registry, certificate holder, and model-scope checks. The business proposition is revealed as the conclusion.
2. **Evidence seam:** a supplier claim and official record initially align; dragging or scrolling across the seam reveals where they split and why the outcome is a discrepancy.
3. **Verification map:** methodology nodes connect in a traceable path. Selecting a node reveals what is checked, why it matters, source type, and possible results.

Additional interactions include contextual desktop cursor labels, evidence metadata reveals, precise status transitions, and reduced-motion/mobile equivalents. No animation suggests a real live database connection.

## Motion language

- **Verified:** position stabilises; borders resolve; status locks into place.
- **Unable to verify:** metadata softens but remains readable; no theatrical disappearance.
- **Discrepancy:** aligned records separate along the evidence seam; conflicting fields are underlined.
- Motion uses transform and opacity, is driven by information progress, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Component system

- Evidence dossier
- Evidence row (source, query date, claim, official record, result)
- Status mark (verified / unresolved / discrepancy)
- Claim comparison seam
- Verification map node and relationship line
- Editorial service ledger
- Scope boundary statement
- Honest mail-draft request form

## Pre-build critique

The initial direction risked becoming the familiar warm-paper, serif, red-stamp “dossier” theme. It was revised to a cool archival palette, restrained typography, and a dynamic evidence seam that encodes an actual verification relationship. Stamps are used sparingly as results, never as decoration. The design is specific to supplier verification even without the logo because every major composition contrasts a claim with a traceable source and result.
